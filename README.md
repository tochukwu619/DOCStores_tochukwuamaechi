## Key Findings

1. Top-Line Growth & High Sales Velocity
   - Strong YoY Expansion: Overall revenue achieved 129,494,075 ₹ in Total Sales (+34.4% vs PY) and 122,307,912.60 ₹ in Net Sales (+32.6% vs PY).
   - Order & Volume Surge: Total orders reached 3,510 (+33.2% vs PY) with 7,125 units sold (+32.7% vs PY), showing consistent order growth matching total revenue trajectory.
2. Product Portfolio Concentration
   - High-Value Electronics Drive Revenue: Sales are heavily concentrated in high-ticket tech and consumer electronics
   - The Top 5 products account for approximately 95M ₹ out of the 129.5M ₹ overall total sales (~73% of total revenue).
3. Promotional & Discount Impact
   - Average Discount Escalation: The average discount rate rose to 5.66% (+30.1% vs PY).
   - Discount Tiers by Campaign: Clearance Sale features the heaviest price cuts at 70%.   New Year Special and Weekend Flash Sale both apply 50% discounts.   Summer Sale (20%) and Festive Diwali (10%) operate at lower promotion tiers.
   - Revenue Compression: Looking at the Total Sales vs. Net Sales Relationship scatter plot, transaction paths diverge noticeably into lower Net Sales bands as higher discount tiers are applied, reducing gross margin on premium items.
4. Geographic Distribution
   - Key Tier-1 & Tier-2 Hubs: Primary sales volume is concentrated in high-density metropolitan areas across India, led by cities like Lucknow, Bhopal, Mumbai, Bangalore, Pune, Ahmedabad, and Jaipur.
5. Historical Trend & Recent Drop-off
   - Stable Multi-Year Baseline: Sales trends remained stable from 2020 through mid-2023.
   - Q1 2024 Downturn: A steep sharp drop in sales occurs in Q1 2024. This indicates either incomplete data ingestion for the final quarter or an operational anomaly requiring investigation.
